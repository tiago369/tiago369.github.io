# Open Source

A few things from my [GitHub](https://github.com/tiago369):

- **[Cross-Engine Humanoid Benchmark](https://github.com/tiago369/cross-engine-humanoid-benchmark)** — Benchmarks a 29-DoF Unitree G1 humanoid across MuJoCo and PyBullet on an identical scripted task, then closes the resulting sim-to-sim gap with a PSO-based calibration method (the same approach used in my SoftRobots sim-to-real work). Includes CI, statistical trials, and full reproducibility.
- **[Desktop Monkey Assistant](https://github.com/tiago369/bonzi-buddy)** — A Bonzi-Buddy-style animated assistant that lives on your desktop, answering typed or spoken questions through a fully local LLM (Ollama) — speech-to-text, text-to-speech, no cloud APIs.
- **ROS 2 tooling** — [`ros2-serial-fpga`](https://github.com/tiago369/ros2-serial-fpga) (UART bridge between ROS 2 topics and an FPGA, in VHDL), [`dobot-mgc-ros2-pkg`](https://github.com/tiago369/dobot-mgc-ros2-pkg) (ROS 2 package for the Dobot Magician arm), and [`fastdds_config_files`](https://github.com/tiago369/fastdds_config_files) (Fast DDS configuration for ROS 2).
