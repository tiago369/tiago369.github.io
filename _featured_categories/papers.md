# Papers

Publications from my research work, mostly around soft robotics and ROS 2 tooling.

- **Optimization of soft manipulator simulation using a particle swarm approach** — SoftRobots project. The PSO-based calibration idea from this paper is also what I reused in [Cross-Engine Humanoid Benchmark](https://github.com/tiago369/cross-engine-humanoid-benchmark) to close a MuJoCo/PyBullet sim-to-sim gap.
- **Recurrent neural networks for modeling soft manipulator in simulated and real environments** — SoftRobots project.
- **Seamless Integration of ROS2 Topics and FPGA via UART: Developing an Easy Integration Layer** — see [ros2-serial-fpga](https://github.com/tiago369/ros2-serial-fpga).
- **Comparative analysis in real environment of trajectory controllers on ROS2**
- **Ethics Applied to Development in Robotics**
